##  Live Feature Cards (`v0.1.4` Track)

### Clean-Room Module ABI & Manifest (`reCart.Contracts`)
- **What it does:** Defines the stable binary ABI (`ICartRecompGameModule`, `ICartMachineRuntime`, `ICartMapper` with numeric `MapperId`/`SubmapperId`, `ICartMemoryBus`, `ICartCpuContext`, `ICartPpuController`, `ICartApuSink`, `ICartQuickSaveProvider`, `CartRegionTimingProfile`, `CartRomHeaderParser`, `CartExecutionTierMode`), clean-room recipe/header helpers (`CartAotRecipeSpec`, `CartPrgHeaderSynthesizer`), identity constants (`EngineId = "reCart"`, `LegacyAliasEngineIds = ["reCartOne", "reNes"]`, `PlatformId = "nes"`, `HostProtocolVersion = "0.6.2"`, `MinSuiteVersion = "0.6.0"`, `UmbrellaSubpath = "StreamItAll\\reEngine\\engines\\reCart\\..."`, `NtscFrameRateHz = 60.0988`, `AllowAutoStart = false`), and `CartPackageManifest`.
- **Verification:** `ContractsAndManifestTests.cs` (constants, 3-way alias metadata, ROM header parsing, and manifest validation).

### Hardware-Timed `2A03` CPU, Mappers `0–3`, Dot `2C02` PPU, APU & Cycle-Paced `CartMachineHost` (`reCart.Core`)
- **What it does:** Executes cycle-stepped `Cpu2A03Core` (`56` official opcodes, zero BCD, `JMP ($xxFF)` page-wrap, `OAM DMA` + `DMC` stalls, `$4016/$4017` open-bus), `Nrom`/`Mmc1`/`UxRom`/`CnRom` mappers via `CartMapperFactory`, dot-stepped `Ppu2C02Core` (`256x240`, `262` scanlines, Loopy `v`/`t`/`x`/`w`, sprite-0 hit & NMI edge gating), `Apu2A03Core` 5-channel audio, cycle-paced `CartMachineHost` (`StepCpuAndApuCycle`, `_pendingAotNmi` `0xFFFA`, `_pendingAotIrq` `0xFFFE`) / `CartQuickSaveSerializer`, and quarantined `InterpreterOracle`.
- **Verification:** `Cpu6502Tests.cs`, `Mapper0To3Tests.cs`, `Ppu2C02AndApuTests.cs`.

### `6502 -> C#` Static Lifter, `recipe.JumpTables`, Cold AOT Cache & `reCart.Decomp` (`reCart.Compiler`, `reCart.Decomp`)
- **What it does:** `Lifter6502ToCSharp`, `LifterJumpTableScanner` (`DiscoverInlineJumpTableTargets` reading `recipe.JumpTables` across fixed and switchable banks), and `CleanRoomRecompPackager` emit and validate strictly 2-member recipe (`module.recomp.json`, `aot-recipe.json`) and 3-member lifted (`module.recomp.json`, `lifted/CartLiftedModule.cs`, `lifted/dispatch.meta.json`) clean-room `.recomp` ZIP archives with zero bundled binaries or retail ROM data; `ColdAotCacheCompiler` builds `<dataDir>\cache\<moduleId>.Generated.dll` out-of-process (`--disable-build-servers -p:UseSharedCompilation=false`, async behind `HostAotCompileOverlayView`), and throws fail-closed `UnliftedPcTrapException`; `CartDecompPlatformProvider` validates clean-room bounds and enforced source quality (`SixTellDetector`, `D4BuildReportValidator` v1+v2 byte-match keys, `CartCleanRoomValidator`).
- **Verification:** `CompilerAndAotCacheTests.cs`, `DecompD4ValidationTests.cs`, `RecipeAndLockstepTests.cs`.

### `reEngine.Host v0.6.2` Player, `.7z` Loader, Clean-Room Media Gate, `< 2 MB` Launcher & Installer (`reCart.Player`, `reCart.Launcher`, `reCart.Installer`)
- **What it does:** `ReCartSessionHost` + `ReCartSessionHost.MediaGateHandlers` + `CartMediaArchiveLoader` + `PlayerCliOptionHelpers` ride `reEngine.Host v0.6.2` (`Foundation v0.5.5`) across all 5 Windows backends (`opengl`, `vulkan`, `directx`, `parallelCpu`, `software`), `WASAPI` audio, 5 aspect presets (`nativePar` default), immediate window launch with async `HostAotCompileOverlayView`, `HostSettingsStore` `SettingsDataDirectory` live `F1` Video/Audio persistence, live `0/8/16 px` `OverscanCrop` drawer refresh (`UpdateDrawerDescriptor`), silent-idle `--demo-cart` audio with input chime, jitter-free `0 px` `F3` HUD, full CLI superset (`--doctor`, `--tray-only`, `--fps-mode`, `--target-fps`, `--vsync`/`--no-vsync`, `--fast-forward`, `--open-settings`, `--show-fps`, `--enable-logs`, `--force-zero-rwx`, `--profile`, `--disc`), `--lockstep-oracle --lockstep-steps`, `<DataDir>\rom\` + `.7z`/`.zip` discovery, interactive Clean-Room Media Gate (`reCart v0.1.4` visibility, `OnBrowseMediaRequested`, `OnBrowseModuleRequested`, `OnFileDropped`, `OnDemoCartRequested`, ROM staging, `UnliftedPcTrapException` trap catch), and Core/Player hot-path optimizations (`PackedRgbaLut`, `MemoryMarshal.Cast`, cached tables, `stackalloc` ports, cached `SDL3.dll` exports); `reCart.Launcher` (`< 2 MB` `reCart.exe`) strips `--autostart*`, detaches interactive GUI launches (`LauncherArgsHelper`), and binds `KILL_ON_JOB_CLOSE` during sync waits; `reCart.Installer` (`"suiteId": "reEngine"`, `ReCartSuitePlacementHelper`, `IsReEngineDirectory`) installs cleanly into `engines\reCart\v0.1.4\` (`app\tools\{7z.exe,7z.dll,LICENSE-7ZIP.txt}`) with atomic `.ready` and Release `ExitCode = 2` on both Player and Setup.
- **Verification:** `PlayerQuickSaveAndInputTests.cs`, `PlayerV013PrelaunchTests.cs`, `InstallerManifestAndSideBySideTests.cs`, totaling **`125/125` isolated tests** across `10` test suites (`0` warnings).
